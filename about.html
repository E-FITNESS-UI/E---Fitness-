Get to know us

We got tired of guessing our own calories.

It started as an annoying observation, we try to count the calories of the food we eat, we get an error message, and we end up not getting a proper response. Sad, aint it?

But the truth is; those apps weren't built for us in mind. that's where we got the idea!

How this started

As everyday people, we are either trying to gain or loose weight, or rather stay in shape and remain fit, to do that we need to check the nutritional value of our food intake, to know wether we are getting in the right amount of calories, fats, proteins and other nutrients, well other apps dont offer that for our local foods, i can't try tracking my amala and ewedu and getting an accurate log of what i'm about to eat. thats where we come in, the app we are building, it's really simple, just take a pic of what you are eating or describe what your eating, and get the accurate log of what you are about to eat, isn't that geart?

We're not adding African food to an existing app. We're starting there.
— E-Fitness
