Our mission

Your fufu deserves the same accuracy as a Hamburger!

A nutrition label shouldn't depend on where you're eating from. We're building the data layer that we have never had before.

The food you grew up eating deserves the same accuracy as a granola bar's nutrition label.
That's the whole premise of E-Fitness.

What "accuracy" actually means to us

Not a rough estimate from an AI model guessing at ingredients — a real, sourced, verifiable number behind every dish.

Representation

Real dishes, real names

Egusi, moin moin, ewa agoyin; logged as what they actually are, with regional variants included, not forced into a Western substitute.

Accuracy

Sourced, not guessed

Every dish is built from the West African Food Composition Table and cross-checked, with a confidence level attached.

Access

Built with the community

We're crowdsourcing dishes directly from the people who cook and eat them, so the database keeps growing beyond what any one team could gather alone.
